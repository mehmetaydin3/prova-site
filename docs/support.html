Prova

Support

Email support@provamusic.app with your device, what you were doing and what happened. A person reads every message.

Password reset#

Choose Forgot your password? on sign-in. Prova emails you a code; enter it in the app to set a new password.

Join your teacher#

Tap your teacher's invite link, or enter your teacher's class code, then wait for your teacher to approve you.

Deleting your account#

Open Profile, then Delete account. It removes your account and what is attached to it, including your takes and sheet-music files. A deleted account can't be restored.

Privacy and safety#

Takes are private until you share them with your teacher. To report a message or comment, press and hold it and choose Report. You can block someone from the same menu. Privacy questions: privacy@provamusic.app. Read the privacy policy and the terms of service.

Beta access#

The beta is for students 13 and older, on iPhone and iPad through TestFlight. Free during the beta. When pricing arrives, teachers pay; students and parents never do.

General enquiries: hello@provamusic.app.
