Prova ("Prova," "we," "us," or "our") is an iPhone and iPad music education application, operated by Mehmet Aydin, an individual based in California, United States. It helps teachers and students organize music, practice, record performances, and exchange feedback.

We believe that a student's creative work should remain under their control. We design Prova to collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide the service, protect private recordings, and give users meaningful control over their information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, who may access it, and your privacy rights.

Prova is currently intended for eligible users in the United States who are at least 13 years old.

1. Who Can Use Prova

Prova is intended for individuals aged 13 and older.

We do not knowingly permit children younger than 13 to create accounts. During registration, we request a date of birth to determine eligibility and enforce our minimum age requirement.

If we discover that an account was created by a child under 13, we will disable the account and delete associated personal information in accordance with applicable law, subject to legally required retention.

If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us at privacy@provamusic.app.

Users aged 13–17 receive additional protections where required by applicable law. We do not use minors' personal information for targeted advertising, sell their personal information, or use their information to train artificial intelligence models.

Prova does not represent that it provides parental monitoring or supervision of private music lessons. Parents and guardians do not have accounts today. They should review the service with teenagers in their care and may contact us at privacy@provamusic.app with any concern about a child's account.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information as necessary to operate Prova.

Account information - Name - Email address - Date of birth - Teacher or student role - Account authentication information - Account and teacher–student relationship identifiers

Your date of birth is used for age eligibility and is not displayed to teachers or other students.

Music and educational content - Uploaded sheet music, scans, PDFs, and other music files - Personal annotations and markings - Practice assignments and instructional feedback - Audio recordings and performances ("Takes") - Practice session durations and related progress information - Shared YouTube links

Teacher–student relationships

We maintain the information necessary to connect teachers and students, deliver assignments, share authorized content, and manage those relationships.

Safety and support information

When you contact support or submit a report, we may collect the information you provide, relevant account identifiers, and information needed to investigate and resolve the issue.

Information from your device - Microphone: used only when you record a take or use the tuner. The tuner processes sound on your device and does not record it. - Notifications: if you turn them on, Apple provides a device token so we can send them. You can turn them off in iOS Settings. - Dictation: if you dictate a note, Apple's speech recognition converts it to text under Apple's privacy terms. - Calendar: used only if you choose to add a lesson to your calendar. - Files and camera: only the files or scans you choose to add.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers or crash reports.

Technical and operational information

Our infrastructure providers may process limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the service, such as IP addresses, authentication events, timestamps, server request information, error records, and security logs.

We do not use third-party advertising trackers or behavioral analytics tools. Operational logs used for security, delivery, or troubleshooting are not used to build advertising profiles.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  1. Create and maintain accounts.
  2. Enforce age eligibility and account security.
  3. Connect teachers and students.
  4. Store, display and manage music and practice materials.
  5. Record and manage performances.
  6. Deliver assignments, annotations and feedback.
  7. Track practice activity and provide related functionality.
  8. Send essential service communications.
  9. Investigate reports, prevent abuse, and protect users.
  10. Maintain, troubleshoot and secure the service.
  11. Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We limit our use of personal information to these purposes and other purposes that are appropriately disclosed and permitted by law.

We do not sell personal information, provide third parties with personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use personal content to train AI models.

4. Your Music, Recordings and Privacy

Your recordings are private by default.

A teacher does not automatically gain access to every recording you create. A recording becomes accessible to an authorized teacher when you choose to share it through the application's sharing controls.

Teachers may also access information and materials associated with their authorized teaching relationships, including assignments and feedback. Certain student-added music may be available to a linked teacher on a read-only basis, where the application expressly indicates that access.

Once a teacher has approved your link, your teacher can see: your profile (never your date of birth), the assignments they give you, pieces you add yourself (read-only, and Prova tells you so), your practice sessions, stats and ratings, takes you choose to share, comments, marked spots, markings on the score, and lesson notes. Other students cannot see your takes, pieces or practice.

Personal annotation layers are managed according to their ownership and sharing permissions.

We do not make student recordings publicly searchable through Prova.

Other people may retain copies of content that they lawfully accessed before a sharing permission was revoked, including through downloads or external recordings outside Prova's control.

5. Teacher–Student Relationships and Blocking

Teachers can access student information only to the extent permitted by the application and the relevant teaching relationship.

When either a student or a teacher blocks the other, the teacher loses access in Prova to that student's assignments, pieces, files, recordings, comments and lessons, including past ones, and can no longer contact the student through Prova. The student keeps their own content.

Blocking does not necessarily delete all underlying records or erase copies that were previously exported or otherwise retained outside Prova.

Records may be retained where necessary for safety investigations, legal obligations, disputes or other legitimate purposes permitted by law.

Users may request deletion of eligible personal information and content as described below.

6. Reporting and Safety

Users can report inappropriate conduct, content, or other safety concerns.

Reports are received and reviewed by the Prova team (or service providers acting on our behalf), not by the reported person's teacher or classmates.

We do not ordinarily disclose the reporting user's identity to the person being reported.

However, absolute anonymity cannot be guaranteed in every circumstance. The facts of an incident, information previously shared between users, or disclosures required by law may make a reporter identifiable.

We restrict access to reports to those who need the information to investigate, enforce our rules, protect users, or meet legal obligations.

We may preserve relevant evidence for a reasonable period where necessary to investigate safety incidents or comply with law.

We use service providers to operate Prova.

Supabase

Provides database, authentication and storage infrastructure. Personal information and user content may be processed and stored through Supabase's infrastructure and authorized subprocessors.

Resend

Processes email addresses and information necessary to deliver account-related and service communications.

Apple

Provides the App Store and TestFlight, iOS platform functionality, push notification infrastructure, optional dictation, and calendar access if you use it. Information processed by Apple is also subject to Apple's applicable privacy terms.

YouTube

When you choose to open or play linked YouTube content, Google/YouTube may receive information relating to that interaction according to its own privacy policies.

We do not control YouTube's independent data practices.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, investigate abuse, protect users, or facilitate a lawful corporate transaction, subject to applicable privacy protections.

We do not authorize service providers to use Prova user content for their own advertising or AI-model training.

The categories and identities of service providers will be updated if our data-processing arrangements materially change.

8. Data Storage and Security

Prova uses cloud infrastructure to store account information and application content.

Primary hosting location: United States (West US).

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information, including access restrictions and infrastructure security controls.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

We limit access to personal information according to operational requirements and implement controls appropriate to the nature of the information.

Where third-party providers process information, we rely on applicable contractual, technical and organizational protections.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Account information is ordinarily retained while an account is active.

Music, recordings, annotations, assignments and practice records are retained as needed to provide the functionality requested by users and maintain authorized teaching relationships.

When an account is deleted, associated personal information and user-generated content will be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable law, except where retention is legally required or otherwise permitted.

Residual copies may remain temporarily in backups or protected operational systems before expiration or deletion under the applicable retention schedule.

Backup retention: We do not currently keep scheduled database backups. Manual backups taken before maintenance are encrypted and deleted within 30 days. If we add scheduled backups, we will update this policy with their retention period.

Account deletion completion period: When you delete your account in the app, your profile, recordings, sheet-music files and related content are removed from our active systems right away.

Security logs and report retention: Our infrastructure providers keep operational and security logs for their own limited periods. We keep safety reports, and the copy of reported content, for as long as needed to review and resolve the report and for up to one year after it is resolved, unless the law requires longer.

Information retained for legal, security or dispute purposes will be restricted to those purposes and deleted when no longer necessary.

10. Your Privacy Rights

You may exercise available privacy rights through the application or by contacting privacy@provamusic.app.

Depending on applicable law, your rights may include:

  • Accessing personal information we maintain about you.
  • Correcting inaccurate account information.
  • Deleting your account and associated eligible personal information.
  • Removing or requesting removal of content you have shared.
  • Receiving a copy of certain personal information.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Appealing a denied privacy request where required by applicable law.

We do not discriminate against users for exercising legally protected privacy rights.

We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity before fulfilling requests and may decline or limit requests where permitted by law.

Where required, we will explain the basis for a denial and provide an appropriate appeal procedure.

Privacy requests: privacy@provamusic.app

11. Additional Protections for Users Under 18

We recognize that teenagers deserve strong privacy protections.

For known minor users, Prova is designed to limit unnecessary data collection, restrict access to personal content, and avoid advertising-based exploitation.

We do not knowingly use minors' personal information for targeted advertising, sell their personal information, or profile them for decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.

We do not intentionally design engagement features to induce excessive or compulsive use by minors.

We do not require precise geolocation to provide Prova's core functionality.

Users under 18 may request removal of eligible content or information they posted or shared through Prova, without necessarily deleting their entire account.

Removal from Prova does not guarantee deletion of copies already obtained or retained independently by others, and certain legal exceptions may apply.

California minors may exercise the removal rights available under California Business and Professions Code section 22581 by contacting privacy@provamusic.app or using applicable in-app removal controls.

12. State-Specific Privacy Rights

Residents of certain U.S. states may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

These may include rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a portable copy of personal information, as well as rights to opt out of certain processing activities.

Prova does not sell personal information or use personal information for targeted advertising as those practices are described in this policy.

Where a state privacy statute applies to Prova, we will honor requests and provide any required appeal process within applicable legal timeframes.

Residents may submit privacy requests to privacy@provamusic.app.

Additional statutory rights and notices will be provided where applicable.

13. Account Deletion

You may initiate deletion of your Prova account within the application.

Deleting your account terminates your access to the service and initiates deletion of associated personal information and user-generated content, subject to applicable legal exceptions and retention requirements.

Information may remain temporarily in backups, or for longer where its retention is legally required.

The app confirms when you have deleted your account.

You may also contact privacy@provamusic.app for help with a deletion request.

14. International Availability

Prova's current beta is intended for eligible users located in the United States.

The service is not currently marketed or offered for general international availability.

Because service providers may use subprocessors in different jurisdictions, the physical location of all processing may not be limited to the United States.

Any material expansion of Prova's geographic availability or international data-processing practices will be assessed for applicable legal requirements and reflected in this policy.
