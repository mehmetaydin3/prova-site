Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Prova is a music-practice app. A teacher sets practice, a student practises and records it, and the teacher listens and replies. This policy explains what information Prova collects to do that, who can see it, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • Prova is for people 13 and older.
  • We collect what the app needs to work: your account details, your practice, the recordings you make, and the messages you exchange with your teacher.
  • No ads. No analytics or tracking tools. We don't sell or share your information, and we don't use it to train AI models.
  • Your recordings are private unless you share them with your teacher.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

2. What we collect

When you create an account

Information Why
Email address and password To sign you in. Your password is stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider; nobody at Prova can see it.
Display name So your teacher (or your students) know who you are.
Date of birth To check that you're 13 or older, which Prova requires. We store the date where only you can read it, and derive an age band (13–17, or adult). We don't use your birthday for anything else.
Role (student or teacher) To show you the right version of the app.
Time zone Read from your device so that "today's practice" and your streak follow your own day, not a server's.

Optional: an instrument and a short bio, if you add them to your profile. Prova has no profile photos.

When you use Prova

  • Practice sessions — how long you practised, when, how it was logged (timer, typed in afterwards, or a performance run), any notes you write, and the ratings you give a session.
  • A daily goal, if you set one, and the streaks, levels and badges Prova works out from your practice.
  • Assignments and pieces — what your teacher sets you (or you set yourself), their checklists and due dates, and a teacher's progress ratings.
  • Recordings — audio you choose to record. Every recording is uploaded to private storage so it's safe and available on your other devices. A recording is visible only to you unless you share it with your teacher. Performance runs are shared with your teacher when you finish them, because that's what they're for; you can make any recording private again.
  • Comments and reactions on a recording, from you or your teacher.
  • Messages between a student and their teacher about an assignment.
  • Sheet music a teacher attaches — a PDF file, or printed pages scanned with the camera (we keep the resulting PDF, not photos) — and any markings either of you draw on it.
  • Lessons a teacher schedules — times, a location or meeting link they type in, and lesson notes.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, a device token so we can send them, and a list of your recent notifications in the app. A notification can include the sender's name and the first part of a message.

Things Prova does on your device only

These never leave your phone or tablet:

  • the tuner and metronome (the tuner listens to your instrument through the microphone in real time; nothing is recorded or sent);
  • recordings you've starred, unsent practice waiting for a connection, and your appearance setting;
  • crash and performance reports, which stay on the device.

Prova only uses the microphone while you're recording, using the tuner, or (for teachers) dictating a note. It only uses the camera when a teacher scans sheet music. Adding a lesson to your calendar only adds events — Prova never reads your calendar.

Information collected automatically

When the app talks to our servers, the servers record technical details such as your IP address, the time, and the app and system version. We use these only to run the service and keep it secure, and they're deleted after [LOG DAYS] days.

3. What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics tools in the app.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information, as those terms are used in US privacy laws, and we never give it to data brokers.
  • No AI training on anything you write, practise or record.
  • No public profiles. There is no public feed, no search for other students, and students can't see or message each other.

4. Who can see your information

  • You can see everything in your account.
  • Your teacher, once you've joined their studio, can see your display name, instrument, bio, age band and time zone; your practice history, goal and streak; your assignments and checklists; recordings you've shared with them; and your messages with them. They can't see your date of birth or your private recordings.
  • Your students (if you're a teacher) can see your display name, profile, the assignments and lessons you create for them, and your messages, comments and reactions.
  • Nobody else. This is enforced by the database itself, not just the app: every table has access rules, and we run automated tests that try to read other people's data and must fail. Audio and sheet music are stored privately and served only to people allowed to see them, through links that expire after an hour.

Keeping conversations appropriate. Messages and comments are automatically checked against a list of inappropriate words. A message that matches is flagged for the teacher in that conversation to review.

Reporting and blocking. You can report any message or comment someone else sent you. The report — your reason, any note you add, and a copy of the reported text and who wrote it — goes to the Prova team, not to the person you reported, and we review it within 24 hours. You can also block someone, which ends your teacher–student link: they can't message you or send you assignments. Blocks are listed under Profile → Blocked people, where you can undo them.

Apart from the word filter and reports you choose to send us, nothing and nobody else reads your messages.

  • Supabase — our database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in the United States (AWS us-west-1, Northern California).
  • Apple — delivers push notifications. If a teacher uses voice dictation, Apple's speech recognition may process that audio to turn it into text; Prova keeps only the text.
  • [EMAIL PROVIDER] — sends sign-up confirmation and password-reset emails.

YouTube. If your teacher attaches a YouTube video and you press play, the video loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com), so Google receives your IP address and information about the video playback under Google's privacy policy. The player can't navigate anywhere else, and it keeps no cookies between videos. If you don't play the video, nothing is sent to YouTube.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

6. How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep your information for as long as your account is open.

Delete your account at any time: Profile → Delete account. (If you've just signed up and haven't chosen a role yet, the same button is on that screen.) Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your sign-in, profile and date of birth;
  • your practice history, goal, streaks and badges;
  • your assignments, checklists, messages, comments and reactions;
  • your recordings, including the audio files, and any sheet music you uploaded;
  • your lessons, notification list and device tokens.

It happens straight away and can't be undone. A few things work differently:

  • If you're a teacher, the assignments and lessons you created for your students are deleted with your account, along with their messages. Your students keep their own practice history and recordings.
  • Notifications already delivered to someone else — for example, "New message from you" in your teacher's notification list — can stay in their list until they clear it.
  • Reports. If someone reported a message you sent, the report keeps its copy of that message after you delete your account, for [REPORT DAYS] days, so we can finish reviewing it — and longer only where the law requires us to preserve it. Reports you filed stay too, without your name.
  • Backups of our database are kept for [BACKUP DAYS] days and then overwritten. We don't restore deleted accounts from them.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See your information: it's all in the app. For a copy in a portable format, email [SUPPORT EMAIL].
  • Correct it: Profile → Edit profile, or ask your teacher to fix anything they created.
  • Delete it: Profile → Delete account, as above.
  • Notifications: turn them off at any time in your device's Settings.
  • Recordings: private by default; you choose which ones your teacher hears, and you can delete any of them.

Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU and UK), you may have further rights — to know what we hold, to get a copy, to correct or delete it, or to object to how it's used. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL]; we'll answer within 30 days, and we won't treat you differently for asking.

8. Children

Prova is for people aged 13 and over. Everyone gives a date of birth when they sign up, and anyone under 13 can't continue — they're asked to delete the account on the spot, and they can do it in one tap.

We don't knowingly keep information about children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using Prova, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we'll delete the account and its information promptly.

9. Security

Your information travels to our servers over encrypted connections, access is enforced by the database for every request, and audio and sheet music are kept in private storage. No system is perfect; if you find a problem, please tell us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
