## 6. Recordings and messages: what we do and do not do

This is the plain-English version. The Privacy Policy is the full version. **CHECK:** the policy
draft must say the same thing in every line below.

**What we do**
- We store your takes (recordings), practice notes, messages, comments, sheet music and score marks
  so Prova works for you.
- We show a take to your teacher only if you choose to share it. A take you do not share stays
  private to you. (Privacy facts § g: a teacher can read only takes marked shared with the teacher.)
- Your teacher can see your practice log, your assignments, and the pieces you add yourself. Prova
  tells students this in the app.
- A take waits on your phone, marked "Not saved yet", until you save it. Nothing uploads until you do.
- We scan messages for language that does not belong and flag it for review (privacy facts § f).
- Your teacher gets a notification when something needs them, and so do you. A notification can
  show the first part of a message. **CHECK:** what a notification may show on a lock screen for a
  teen (privacy facts, item 9).

**What we do not do**
- We do not sell your recordings or messages.
- We do not use them for ads, and we do not track you across other apps or sites.
- We do not use them to train AI models. **CHECK:** nothing in the code does, but the founder must
  confirm this is a promise he wants to make in a contract.
- We do not show your takes or messages to other students. In this beta there is no feed and no
  way for students to see each other.
- We do not read your private takes as a matter of routine. **CHECK:** who at Prova can technically
  open a recording or message (the founder, through the database), and when, for example to review
  a report. Counsel decides how this is worded.

**How long we keep them.** Until you delete them or your account. **CHECK:** the code has no
automatic expiry for takes, messages or notes (privacy facts § f). The founder may decide a rule.
