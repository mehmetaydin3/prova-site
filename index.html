MADE FOR THE PEOPLE AROUND THE MUSIC

One practice loop.
More room to play.

Focused tools for musicians, and a clearer connection between the lesson and the week that follows.

01 / TEACHERS

Hear the week.
Shape what comes next.

Set practice steps and a daily target. Listen to the takes students choose to share, then leave feedback on the score.

02 / STUDENTS

Open the music.
Know where to begin.

Keep your score, metronome, tuner and recorder together. Bring your own pieces, too—even when you practice on your own.

03 / PARENTS

Private by default.
Calm by design.

Takes are private unless a student shares them with their teacher. The beta is for musicians age 13 and up.

04 / HOBBYISTS

Your music.
Your own rhythm.

Use Prova’s practice tools for music you choose yourself. A linked teacher can see student-added pieces read-only, as the app explains.

Prova is an iPhone and iPad app. Parent accounts and parent-link features are not available in this beta.

A DIFFERENT KIND OF PRACTICE APP

Practice has its tools.
Teaching needs a thread.

Practice companions and studio platforms do useful, different work. Prova is built around the space between them: the music a teacher assigns is the music a student practices, records and brings back for feedback.

01 SOLO PRACTICE

Tools for the stand.

TonalEnergy·Modacity

Tuner, metronome, recording and practice reflection help musicians work on their own.

Great for the individual practice session.

02 STUDIO MANAGEMENT

Tools around the studio.

My Music Staff

Scheduling, student records, lesson resources and billing help teachers organize studio work.

Great for the running of a teaching studio.

03 PROVA

The lesson, carried into practice.
