01 The sound of progress

A take, heard. A moment, answered.

Maya · Minuet in G, bars 5–8 · take 30:00
0:18 · her teacherLet the half note ring its full value.

Tuesday. Maya practises bars 5 to 8 with the score, the timer and the metronome in reach.

A take. She records it and shares it with her teacher. Takes stay private until she chooses.

Between lessons, her teacher listens with the waveform in view.

At 0:18 she stops the playhead and drops a comment on that moment.

The note lands on the second it's about: Let the half note ring its full value.

Maya opens the take. The note sits at 0:18, a tap plays the moment, and she knows exactly what to try next.

02 The music between us

Two layers. One score.

Menuet in G · BWV Anh. 114 · bars 5–8
Teacher's viewMineStudentBothSaved

The teacher opens the score Maya is practising.

Bars 5 to 8: the phrase that needs a thought.

A circle around the F♯, in the teacher's own layer. Then Save.

Maya opens the same score. The mark is there, in the Teacher layer, exactly where it was drawn.

She answers in her own layer: a bracket under bar 6. Save.

Back with the teacher: Both layers, one score. Marks arrive when they're saved, not while someone is drawing.

Who it's for

One loop, four people.

Teachers

Send the week's work: steps, a daily target, scans, PDFs or a YouTube link. Hear the takes students share and see their practice time. Leave the next thought on the score, in your own layer.

Invite with a link or a code; you approve who joins.

Students

Open Today and know where to start. Practise with the score, the metronome and the tuner on one stand. Record a take, keep it or share it. Keep your own marks beside your teacher's: Mine, Teacher or Both.

Add your own pieces, too.

Parents

Your child sees their teacher's guidance on the score and knows what to practise next. Takes stay private unless they share them with their teacher. Parents never pay to use Prova.

Independent musicians

No teacher yet? Add the pieces you're working on, practise with Today, the metronome and the tuner, and keep your takes. When a teacher invites you, their guidance joins your music.
