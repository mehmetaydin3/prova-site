02 The music between us

Two layers. One score.

Alex · teacher MineStudentBoth Saved

Alex opens Mark up on the score Maya is practising.

Bars 12 to 16, the phrase that closes the first half.

A circle around the ornament in bar 13, in Alex’s own layer. Then Save.

Maya opens the same score. The circle is in her Teacher layer, exactly where it was drawn. That layer is Alex’s; her pens stay away.

She answers in her own layer: a bracket under the runs in bars 14 and 15. Save.

Back with Alex: Mine, Student or Both on one score. Marks arrive when they’re saved, not while someone is drawing.

  1. Teacher marks. Alex circles the ornament in bar 13, in their own layer, and saves.

  2. Student sees. Maya opens the score; the circle is in her Teacher layer.

  3. Student marks. She answers in her own layer, under bars 14 and 15, and saves.

  4. Teacher sees. Back with Alex: both layers on one score, synced when saved.

Who it’s for

One loop, four kinds of musician.

Teachers

Send the week’s work with steps, a daily target, scans, PDFs or a YouTube link. Hear the takes students share and answer at the exact second. Leave the next thought on the score, in your own layer.

Invite with a link or a code; you approve who joins.

Students

Open Today and know where to start. Practise with the score, the metronome and the tuner in one place. Record a take, then keep it private or share it with your teacher. Your marks sit beside your teacher’s: Mine, Teacher or Both.

Add your own pieces, too.

Parents

Your child sees their teacher’s guidance on the score and knows what to practise next. Takes stay private unless they share them with their teacher. Parents never pay for Prova.

Students 13 and up during the beta.

Independent musicians

No teacher yet? Add the pieces you’re working on, practise with the metronome and the tuner, and keep your takes. When you join a teacher, their guidance joins your music.
