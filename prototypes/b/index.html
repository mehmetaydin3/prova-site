Music practice, for the week between lessons

A lesson lasts an hour. The music gets learned in the week in between.

Prova keeps that week connected. The teacher sends the work. The student practices it, records the bar that's giving them trouble, and shares it. The teacher listens and answers on that exact moment. By the next lesson, you're both starting from the same place.

A note for parents

What Prova knows about your child, and who sees it.

Music students skew young, and a recording of a child's voice is personal information. We designed Prova around that, from the data model up, and we'd rather tell you plainly than make you read a policy to find out.

Prova stores your child's name and email, their practice log, and the takes they choose to share. Their teacher sees those. Nobody else does. There is no public feed, no leaderboard, no way for a stranger to reach them: students join with a class code and the teacher approves each one.

There are no ads and no third-party analytics. Nothing is sold or profiled. Data lives in the United States. Your child can delete the account, recordings and history from Profile at any time, and so can you with them.

The beta is for students 13 and up. The plan is for younger students to join through a parent, with verifiable consent, before public launch. A parent view, where you hear the take and see the week, is being built.
