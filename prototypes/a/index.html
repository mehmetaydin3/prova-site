Schematic. Real screen recordings replace these four screens.

01 · Teacher

Send the week's work once.

A few words on what to focus on, a checklist of steps, a tempo, a YouTube link and the sheet music, scanned with the camera or a PDF from Files. To one student, or all of them. Save it as a template, and next week takes a tap.

02 · Student

Practice it in one place.

Score, timer, metronome and recorder together, with the teacher's note in view. The practice screen is the calmest screen in the app: no streaks, no badges, nothing that competes with the music.

03 · Student

Record the bar that's giving them trouble.

Twenty seconds is enough. A take stays private until the student chooses to share it, and sharing means the teacher, never a feed.

04 · Teacher

Answer the exact moment.

Play the take and stop where it matters. The note is pinned to that second, and the student taps it to hear exactly what you mean. Short on time? A quick response takes one tap.

Feedback on the recording

Hear the practice, not just the lesson.

Scroll to move through the take. The teacher's notes sit on the seconds they're about, and the student taps a note to hear that moment again, at full speed or half.

Safety and privacy

Built for a studio of kids.

Music students skew young, and a recording of a child's voice is personal information. Prova is designed around that, from the data model up.

13+

Ages 13 and up in the beta.

The plan is for younger students to join through a parent, with verifiable consent, before public launch. Until then they're screened out at sign-up, kindly.

●

Private by default.

A take stays with the student until they share it, and sharing means their teacher. There is no public feed.

0

No ads. No third-party analytics.

Nothing is sold, profiled or shared with advertisers. Data lives in the United States.

1

One code brings a studio in.

Students join with the teacher's class code, and the teacher approves each one. Nobody can reach a student the teacher didn't admit.

!

Report and block, from any message.

Reports come to us, not to the person reported, and every one is reviewed within 24 hours.

×

Delete everything, any time.

Profile → Delete account removes the account, recordings and practice history straight away.

For parents

What Prova knows, and who sees it.

Your child's name and email, their practice log, and the takes they choose to share. Their teacher sees those. Nobody else does.

Account deletion is in the app, and the full privacy policy is written to be read by a parent, not a lawyer.

Read the privacy policy

Questions parents ask

  • Can strangers contact my child? No. Only the teacher who admitted them, and only inside the studio.
  • Where do the recordings go? To Prova's storage in the United States, visible to your child and, when shared, their teacher.
  • Is there a leaderboard? No. Levels and badges reward showing up, and they're personal, not ranked.
  • Do I need an account? Not for the beta. A parent view, where you hear the take and see the week, is being built.
