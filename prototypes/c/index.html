For students

Safety and privacy

Built for a studio of kids.

Music students skew young, and a recording of a child's voice is personal information. Prova is designed around that, from the data model up. The beta is for students 13 and up; the plan is for younger students to join through a parent, with verifiable consent, before public launch.

For parents

Who sees your child's recordings.

Their teacher, when your child shares a take. Nobody else. Students join with a class code and the teacher approves each one, so nobody your child's teacher didn't admit can reach them.

You don't need an account for the beta. A parent view, where you hear the take and see the week, is being built.

Questions parents ask

  • Can strangers contact my child? No. Only the teacher who admitted them.
  • Is there a leaderboard? No. Levels and badges are personal, not ranked.
  • Can we delete everything? Yes, from Profile, straight away.
  • What about younger kids? The plan is for under-13s to join through you, with verifiable consent, before public launch.
